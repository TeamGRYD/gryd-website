**Studio 54**, a builder of white-label sports-fantasy platforms, proposed an on-chain fantasy programme with **Argentinos Juniors**, the Argentine club founded in 1904 and associated with Diego Maradona. The programme included team management, player trades, competitions and NFT player cards, with the goal of bringing the club's fan base onto Polkadot. Argentinos Juniors would provide press, website placement, social channels and live-event activation.

The request was 440,000 USDT, with milestone payouts tied to monthly active users. **OpenGov rejected it.**
