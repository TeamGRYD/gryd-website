ZUNNO proposed a multiplayer, verifiable UNO game on Polkadot: **ink! smart contracts on the Pop parachain**, a **commit-and-reveal** scheme to keep hands and the deck hidden, optimistic client rollups, and multiple rooms. The request was 50,000 USDT, milestone-based through a multisig. We introduced the team to Polkadot and OpenGov and curated the scope of the proposal.
