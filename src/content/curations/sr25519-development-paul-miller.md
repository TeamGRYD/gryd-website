In August 2024 we offered on GitHub to help Paul Miller, author of the noble cryptography libraries, draft a Polkadot treasury proposal for an sr25519 implementation. We curated **referendum #1165** ("Development of @noble/curves/sr25519 package by Paul Miller and audit sponsorship (1/2)"). It **passed with 100% aye** and was executed on 7 October 2024 (19,800 USDT, paid to the developer).

During curation we took feedback from Jeff Burdges and Shawn Tabrizi: MuSig was removed from scope, and OLAF support was left for a separate proposal.
