SolidityScan approached us to integrate with the Edgeware EdgeEVM explorer. We suggested starting with four EVM-enabled parachains (BiFrost, Moonbeam, Hydration and Unique Network) before an ecosystem-wide rollout. The proposal asked for **11,000 USDC**: a $2,500 integration fee per explorer plus 1,000 USDC for curation and operations.

**OpenGov rejected it** (14.4% aye / 85.6% nay). We publish it because unsuccessful curations are part of an honest track record.
