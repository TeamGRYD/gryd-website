## Why we built it
For the **Road to DevCon 8** campaign before DevCon 8 in Mumbai (3–6 November 2026), we wanted workshops students *do*, not just watch.

## What it does
- **Presentations** for each workshop session.
- **Quizzes and quests** on Ethereum dev tools and the five pillars of CROPS.
- A **leaderboard** and wallet connection.
- A **wiki** with take-away summaries.

It is open source under Apache-2.0 and is actively developed.
