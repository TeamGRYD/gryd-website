The **first edition of the Polkadot India Workshops Series** ran at IIT Roorkee on 19 October 2024, organised by Edgetributor SubDAO (now GRYD) with Blockseblock and the NSS team of IIT Roorkee. The goal was to start Polkadot developer communities across India by teaching at leading institutes.

## What we taught
- **Intro to Web3:** history of blockchains and smart contracts, fundamentals, the basics of Polkadot, governance.
- **Substrate / Polkadot SDK:** types of Substrate chains, pallet development, WASM contracts, VMs in Substrate.
- **JavaScript workshop:** a starter API template, the workshops monorepo, RPC calls and metadata.
- **ink! smart contracts:** taught remotely by Bruno Galvao (R0GUE, founder of Pop Network).

## Highlights
- Peak attendance of **200+ students** against an expected 150–200.
- Two hands-on activities, *Rob the Bank* (from PBA) and a *Decryption Challenge*, with 9 winners.
- All **200 POAPs** claimed during the event.
- We built a **PJS starter template** and a **workshops monorepo** so students could start from one clone.

## What we learned
Every session ran over time, so later editions moved to a **two-day format**. We also learned to promote for four weeks or more, spread topics within each workshop, and add more interactive activities.

## Funding and transparency
The workshops were funded through the DOT Events Bounty. Unused budget (**140.4664 DOT**) was returned, as recorded in our public report. An earlier OpenGov proposal for a combined workshop and hackathon (referendum #1131) was rejected on-chain.
