The **Stellar × Polkadot HackerHouse BLR** (4–6 December 2025, The Hosteller, Indiranagar) was a 2.5-day, fully sponsored build sprint for projects that **combine Stellar's Soroban smart contracts with Polkadot's interoperability architecture**. GRYD co-hosted it with Stellar India, Polkadot and Campustocrypto Community.

## Format
- Accommodation, meals and mentoring covered for selected builders.
- Mentors and judges from **Stellar India** and **Edgetributors / ArQ (MontaQ Labs)**.
- Submissions needed significant new work during the event, a Git history, a 3–5 minute demo video and a project description. They were collected in our open [hackathons monorepo](https://github.com/TeamGRYD/hackathons-monorepo).

## Prizes
- **Builders:** ₹50,000 total (₹15,000 / ₹10,000 / ₹5,000 for the top three, plus ₹20,000 shared among valid submissions).
- **Creators:** $150 for the three best creators.
