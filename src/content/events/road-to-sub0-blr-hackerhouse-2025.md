The **Road to Sub0 India BLR Hackerhouse** (15–18 November 2025) brought builders selected from the Indian participants of the **Polkadot Builder Party hackathon** to Zo House in Whitefield, Bengaluru. It was hosted by Edgetributor SubDAO (now GRYD) and MontaQ Labs, with support from the Polkadot Roots initiative.

## What happened
- **Day 1:** check-in, opening keynote and Polkadot ecosystem session, ideation and mentor matchmaking, hackathon orientation.
- **Day 2:** implementation sprints, a *Building Resilient Substrate Apps* workshop, mentor office hours, milestone demos.
- **Day 3:** final implementation, submission and mentor review, then **Demo Day** pitches to Polkadot organisers and mentors, followed by certificates.
- **Day 4:** check-out.

The hacker house was the finale of our [Road to Sub0 India](/education/meetups/#road-to-sub0-india) meetup series across 11 cities. Luma lists **41 participants**.
