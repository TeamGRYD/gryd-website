Some students who attended our IIT Bombay workshops invited us to **mentor and judge at INNOV8 3.0**, the hackathon at their own college, Terna Engineering College, held on 20–21 March 2025. The college added a dedicated **Polkadot track** and funded its prizes. We sponsored the food.

## What we ran
1. **Mini-workshop 1:** history of Web3 from the Polkadot perspective.
2. **Mini-workshop 2:** Polkadot SDK and deployment modes (pallets, ink!, Frontier EVM).
3. **Mini-workshop 3:** porting contracts to pallet-revive on Westend AssetHub, for the 6 teams building on Polkadot testnets.

Between the workshops we mentored teams, ran a POAP activity, introduced the Students Collective, and judged two rounds.

## Numbers
The organiser report counts **120 attendees** (100 hackers, 16 organising students, 3 faculty coordinators and the principal). Luma shows 175 marked as going.
