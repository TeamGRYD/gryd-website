**DevCon 8 comes to Mumbai on 3–6 November 2026.** On the road to it, GRYD runs the **Road to DevCon 8: Workshops Edition**, a community initiative that introduces engineering students to the Ethereum ecosystem with hands-on work on its dev tools and builder paradigms, and practical quests on the five pillars of CROPS.

The first session took place at **ATLAS SkillTech University**, Kurla West, Mumbai, with **GrowSphere** and **Web3 Mumbai**. It had two workshops, two hands-on activities, quests, quizzes and take-away summaries. Luma lists **206 participants**.

## Built for it: the Road2DevCon portal
We built [road2devcon.quest](https://road2devcon.quest), an open-source education portal with presentations, quizzes, quests, a leaderboard and a wiki, to run these workshops. [More about the portal →](/builders/road2devcon/)
