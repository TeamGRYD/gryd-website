The **JAM Tour Mumbai** brought Dr. Gavin Wood to Mumbai on 13 February 2025 to present JAM, the next evolution of Polkadot. Pala Labs organised the tour, and Edgetributor SubDAO (now GRYD) is listed as a **local partner**.

## Our role
- **Pre-JAM education:** our [IIT Bombay workshops](/education/events/polkadot-india-workshops-iit-bombay-2025/) ran the weekend before, so attendees came prepared for the session.
- **Volunteer co-hosts:** we helped the JAM Tour production team with setup, attendee check-ins and food logistics.

The Luma page shows **286 attendees** marked as going.
