The **second edition of the Polkadot India Workshops Series** took place at IIT Bombay on 8–9 February 2025. We scheduled it just before the **JAM Tour Mumbai**, so students arrived at Dr. Gavin Wood's session already knowing the current Polkadot stack.

## Programme
**Day 1:** history of Web3 (Bitcoin to multichain frameworks), blockchain fundamentals (consensus, cryptography, Merkle trees, light clients), DeFi and NFTs with parachains and AssetHub.

**Day 2:** Polkadot so far and next (relay chain, parachains, OpenGov, Polkadot 2.0, intro to JAM), protocol development (FRAME pallets, ink!, Frontier and Revive EVMs, PJS, PAPI, SubKey), and runtime and appchain development (Polkadot SDK, templates, XCM, preparing for JAM).

## People
- **Hosts:** the Edgetributor SubDAO / GRYD team.
- **Guest lecturers:** Abhiraj Mengade (PBA graduate, Unit Network) and Advait Rane (Indigo, Cardano).
- **Partners:** WnCC (Web and Coding Club, IIT Bombay) as university partner, Web3 Mumbai as community partner.

## Numbers
**405 registrations** on Luma and **300+ attendees** reported, against a target of 300–400 registrations. **291 DOT** of unused budget was returned to the DOT Events Bounty.
