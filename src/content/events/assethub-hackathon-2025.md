The **Polkadot AssetHub Hackathon 2025** was organised by Edgetributor SubDAO (now GRYD) and MontaQ Labs, powered by the DOT Events Bounty. Builders worked on Polkadot AssetHub and PolkaVM smart contracts.

## Format
| Stage | Dates | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Registration closes | 29 May 2025 | Online |
| Opening ceremony | 30 May 2025 | Online |
| Road to AssetHub meetups | 31 May – 1 June 2025 | Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi, Hyderabad, Lucknow |
| Mini-workshops: *Solidity to PolkaVM Decoded*, *Web3 from a Frontend Perspective* | 2–3 June 2025 | YouTube |
| AMA & live demo | 4 June 2025 | YouTube |
| Eliminator round | 30 May – 6 June 2025 | Online |
| **Finale** | **14–16 June 2025** | **Villa Calangute, Goa** |

## Prizes and winners
The prize pool was **$3,000**: $1,500 for first place, $1,000 for second and $500 for third. **First prize went to the PolkaZK Verifier SDK** (Jatin Sahijwani and Anirudh Singh Chouhan), a CLI and SDK that compiles Circom circuits and verifies Groth16 proofs on PolkaVM. After the hackathon we continued to [curate the project](/business/curations/zk-verifier-sdk-zkama/).
