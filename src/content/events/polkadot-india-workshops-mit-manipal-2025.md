The **third edition of the Polkadot India Workshops Series** opened at the Manipal Institute of Technology on 16–17 August 2025, with **IECSE Manipal** as university partner and support from the DOT Events Bounty.

## Agenda
**Day 1:** history of Web3, blockchain fundamentals, DeFi and NFTs on parachains and AssetHub.
**Day 2:** a deep dive into the Polkadot ecosystem (Polkadot 2.0, OpenGov, intro to JAM), protocol development (FRAME, ink!, PolkaVM and Frontier EVMs, PAPI), and runtime and application chain development with the Polkadot SDK and XCM.

Luma shows **215 registrations**. The following weekend, the same edition ran at [NITK Surathkal](/education/events/polkadot-india-workshops-nitk-surathkal-2025/).
