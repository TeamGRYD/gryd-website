ETHIndia 2024 (6–8 December, KTPO, Bengaluru) was **the first time Polkadot sponsored ETHIndia**. Edgetributor SubDAO (now GRYD) represented the Polkadot ecosystem with a Silver-tier sponsorship funded by the DOT Events Bounty.

## What we did
- Hosted an **ink! development workshop** on day 1, with introductions to pallet, runtime and EVM deployment on Polkadot.
- Prepared a **Hackathon Bible** on the booth standee, covering five ways to deploy on Polkadot: ink!, Frontier EVMs, pallet-revive on Westend AssetHub, pallets, and runtimes/appchains.
- Ran the **Polkadot booth** (800+ on-booth interactions reported) and a social quest (320+ posts with #PolkadotAtETHIndia).
- **Judged the Polkadot track.**

## Results
**22 projects** were submitted to the Polkadot track. 16 used Frontier-based EVMs, 7 used Substrate-native attestations, and 2 used ink! contracts.
- **Winner:** MemeCoinGen, a WhatsApp-based token launcher for ink! contracts.
- **1st runner-up:** Khoj, a gamified campaign portal for in-person events.
- **2nd runner-up:** ContractCraft (AI-assisted ink! contract builder) and Placeholder (attestation-based ad reputation).

## Transparency
Unspent funds, **1,723.992 DOT**, were returned to the Events Bounty, with on-chain transactions linked in our report.
