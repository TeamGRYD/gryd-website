The **Polkadot Codecamp India 2026** was a one-day structured training programme, run with **OpenGuild** to prepare participants for the Polkadot Hub hackathon. No prior Polkadot experience was required; basic programming knowledge was recommended. The Bangalore edition (7 February 2026, co-hosted with Zo World) followed the [Mangalore edition](/education/workshops/#polkadot-codecamp-india-2026) on 1 February.

## Topics
- Polkadot Hub architecture and **EVM smart contracts**.
- Developer tooling: DevContainer, Foundry/Hardhat, Scaffold DOT.
- An introduction to **cross-chain apps with XCM**.
- Next steps: hackathons, grants and ecosystem teams.

## Format
On-site and hands-on, with guided setup, live coding and small-group Q&A. Luma lists **116 participants**.
