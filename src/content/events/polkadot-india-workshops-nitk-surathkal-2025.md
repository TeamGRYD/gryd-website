On 23–24 August 2025 the **Polkadot India Workshops Series** came to **NIT Karnataka, Surathkal**, with NITK WebClub and COSH NITK as partners and support from the DOT Events Bounty. With **477 registrations** on Luma, it is the largest edition of the series so far by registrations.

## Agenda
**Day 1:** Web3 history, blockchain fundamentals, DeFi and NFTs.
**Day 2:** Polkadot ecosystem deep dive, protocol development, and runtime and application chain development.

## Hosts
The GRYD team, joined by guest hosts Abhiraj Mengade, Apoorva Agrawal and Rahul Balani.
