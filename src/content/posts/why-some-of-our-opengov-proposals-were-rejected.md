Most organisations only show their wins. We publish every curation, including the ones that didn't pass. Here are the proposals we wrote or curated that were **rejected on-chain**, with links so you can check the votes yourself.

| Proposal | Our role | Result |
|---|---|---|
| [#1131 IIT Roorkee Workshops & Hackathon (1/5)](https://polkadot.subsquare.io/referenda/1131) | Co-proposer with Blockseblock | Rejected, 6 Sep 2024 |
| [#1288 Argentinos Juniors Fantasy (Studio 54)](https://polkadot.subsquare.io/referenda/1288) | Curation & operations | Rejected, 10 Dec 2024 |
| [#1300 SolidityScan explorer integrations](https://polkadot.subsquare.io/referenda/1300) | Curation & operations | Rejected, 19 Dec 2024 |
| [#1520 sr25519 audit sponsorship (2/2)](https://polkadot.subsquare.io/referenda/1520) | Proposer | Rejected **on purpose**: moved to the dotPAL bounty |
| [Kusama #565 PolkArena](https://kusama.subsquare.io/referenda/565) | Co-proposer with MontaQ Labs | Rejected, 2025 |
| [#1751 Retroactive pay for sr25519 coordination](https://polkadot.subsquare.io/referenda/1751) | Proposer | Rejected, 12 Oct 2025 |

## What we took away
These are our own readings of the votes. OpenGov voters rarely explain a nay.

1. **Big Tipper splits don't hide a big budget.** IIT Roorkee was planned as four big tippers plus a small spender. Voters look at the total. We later funded the workshops through the DOT Events Bounty, which fit the size of the request better.
2. **Commercial proposals need clear value for the ecosystem.** Studio 54's fantasy-sports programme with Argentinos Juniors asked for 440,000 USDT against monthly-active-user milestones. Our read: the on-chain activity it promised wasn't enough to justify the treasury spend.
3. **Integrations should come from the chains that benefit.** The SolidityScan request covered four parachains' explorers. Parachain-level budgets may fit better than the relay-chain treasury.
4. **Platforms are a harder sell than events.** PolkArena (a Luma-meets-DoraHacks platform, 125,300 USD) received no aye votes.
5. **Budget coordination up front.** Retroactive funding for our sr25519 coordination failed. Public goods coordination should be in the original proposal.

## What passed
For balance: the faucet (#541, #904), the sr25519 development (#1165, **100% aye**), AgentDOT (Fast-Grants) and our events (DOT Events Bounty) were all funded and delivered. See [all curations](/business/) and [what we've built](/builders/).
