**GRYD is the new name of Edgetributor SubDAO**, a collective of solo contributors from the Edgeware DAO, one of the oldest Substrate projects in the Polkadot ecosystem. Here is how we got here, with links to the public record for each step.

## Edgeware roots
Our contributors started out building for Edgeware. In 2023 an "EdgewareDAO" team built **EdgeSnap**, a MetaMask Snap for Edgeware, at ETHforAll. We kept shipping after the incentives stopped. That became the culture.

## Edgetributor SubDAO (2024–2025)
As a SubDAO we focused on three things: **building, curating and educating**.

- **Building:** the [Universal Hybrid Substrate Faucet](/builders/universal-hybrid-substrate-faucet/), funded by the Polkadot treasury in 2024, and [AgentDOT](/builders/agentdot/), funded by Polkadot Fast-Grants.
- **Curating:** we helped external teams through Polkadot OpenGov. That includes [Paul Miller's sr25519 library](/business/curations/sr25519-development-paul-miller/) and [its security audit](/business/curations/scure-sr25519-audit/), as well as proposals that [didn't pass](/business/).
- **Educating:** the [Polkadot India Workshops Series](/education/workshops/) at IIT Roorkee, IIT Bombay, MIT Manipal and NITK Surathkal; [ETHIndia 2024](/education/events/ethindia-2024-polkadot/); the [AssetHub Hackathon](/education/events/assethub-hackathon-2025/); and the Road to Sub0 India meetups and hacker house.

## Becoming GRYD
<Placeholder id="PH-17">In late 2025 the collective began operating as **GRYD**, and since then our work has also covered Stellar (a cross-ecosystem hacker house) and Ethereum (the Road to DevCon 8 workshops).</Placeholder>

The work hasn't changed: build in the open, curate honestly, teach from first principles. **No hype. Just the work.**
